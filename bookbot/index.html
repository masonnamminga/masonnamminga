The Inspiration

The Bookbot started off as a scrap wood project before I knew anything about woodworking, coding, or electronics, and has now turned into an interactive robot library.

The Results

I don’t know about you, but I think that the Bookbot turned out pretty cool. BRING BOOKS TO KEEP THE LIBRARY STOCKED!

The Future

Each season, the Bookbot is refreshed with new cabinet lighting colors, music, and screen updates. The Halloween display🔗 features ominous music, while Mariah Carey “defrosts” in late November with an iconic Christmas display🔗. The story printer also offers new stories and poems.

Version Five: Where we're at...

An all new (more weatherproof) Bookbot + A PHOTOBOOTH!

Due to a water leak, the Bookbot sustained substantial mold damage during the winter. The new 2026 cabinet has increased waterproofing measures, as well as a smaller footprint. It’s ready for whatever the soggy PNW weather throws at it.

Version One

The Bookbot started off in 2017 when I wanted to create a take-a-book, leave-a-book library for the neighborhood. Constructed from scrap plywood and a moving dolly the “Book Shack” was created.

Version Two

In June of 2021, the Bookbot was remade. It included plastic doors, a larger cabinet, and a robot head with a sound module that would talk when different buttons were pressed.

Version Three

In 2022, the Book Bot was updated with a new cabinet, improved lighting, a head featuring an integrated screen for displaying updates, and speakers that play calming music for passersby.

Version Four

During the Summer of 2024 the Bookbot was upgraded with better waterproofing, new doors, and a new screen. It included a thermal printer that can dispense a poem as well as a photobooth camera.
